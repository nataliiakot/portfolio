## User clarification — 17 November 2026

These facts supersede earlier generic descriptions of an existing business rule or unknown release status:

- Cleanly was designed from scratch to help cleaners and managers find jobs and clients find a cleaner.
- Interviews identified cleaners’ fear of visiting unverified people. Nataliia proposed a personal client network in response, with review before new clients are added.
- The manager carries out the check. The app records the manager’s permission or refusal; do not describe automatic identity checks, document verification, background checks, or guaranteed physical safety.
- The app was implemented and used. The reported −30% covers the time from opening the app to receiving confirmation from the client, based on analytics during the first month and comparison with similar apps the users had used before.
- No participant count for the measurement, raw timings, named comparison apps, or precise comparison protocol has been supplied. Do not infer them from the separate four-product competitive review.
- This is an app-level timing outcome, not an isolated experiment proving that the client network caused the improvement.

Keep the three interview participants separate from the analytics population. Existing product collages and research boards remain available; no old Cleanly UI exists for a before/after redesign comparison because the product was created from scratch.
