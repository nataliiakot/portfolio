# Case study editorial direction

The audience is prospective clients and hiring teams. Each case should explain the central product problem, Natalia's actual contribution, the reasoning behind selected decisions, and the outcome. Keep the site's existing visual language and all supplied imagery.

All six linked cases in Anastasia Bovt's portfolio were read on 2026-11-03:

These are structural references. No wording, research, clients, metrics or imagery from these projects belongs to Natalia's cases.

## Applied stories

- Cleanly: interest versus assignment, manager control, visible status, field preparation and communication. The 30% coordination result was provided by Natalia.
- HR Boost: research, connected modules, consistent patterns and team coordination at 450+ screens. The 45% administration and 7% onboarding results were provided by Natalia.
- Fleeto: overview, investigation and administration; filtering, bulk management, permissions and trip context. The 21% coordination and 15% downtime results were provided by Natalia. Future opportunities remain explicitly separate.
- Nova Bank: everyday financial tasks, clarity, information architecture and control. Outcomes describe design deliverables without inventing launch metrics.
- Fitness App: an interface walkthrough based on supplied screens. Do not claim research, validation, project duration, personal role or business results without additional source material.

The quantitative results above were supplied in conversation, not independently verified. No sample sizes, baselines, interview quotes or test results have been added.

## Editorial rules

Use concise, project-specific headings. State what changed and why before listing features. Show the relevant image beside its explanation, with factual captions when helpful. Distinguish rationale from observed research findings. Keep English and Ukrainian in sync. Preserve all original image assets and Notion source links.

## Follow-up: role, diagrams and Steady

Natalia confirmed that she is the sole lead designer on every project. Every public case now states `Sole Lead Product Designer`, with a corresponding Ukrainian translation. Team-size facts are omitted from the public facts row; historical source data is retained separately from the role statement.

Fitness App is now named **Steady**. Its expanded narrative explains the product direction, design problem, ownership, daily loop, workout hierarchy, guidance, nutrition, visual system and design outcome. The existing `/projects/fitness-app/` route remains stable. No research sample, duration, validation data or numerical business result has been invented for Steady.

Each case has an accessible HTML/CSS diagram with translated labels: Cleanly's assignment branches; HR Boost's shared employee context; Fleeto's monitor/investigate/administer model; Nova Bank's task structure; and Steady's plan/prepare/act/reflect sequence. Previously supplied numerical results are now set in dedicated outcome panels. Key principles use larger type.
